Hit it before it vanishes.

Five short drills for aim, accuracy, focus and reaction time. Windows, macOS and Linux.

Download All platforms

Simple to learn, hard to master

Keep the combo alive

Every 5 hits in a row raises your multiplier, up to x8. Clicking empty space resets it.

Gold heals

Worth 5x points and restores a life.

Bombs bite

Click one and you lose a life. Let it fade.

A pace you can actually keep up with

Classic eases up over the first three minutes, then holds steady. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard.

Five drills, one menu

Classic
Shrinking targets. Build a combo, grab gold, dodge bombs.
Reaction
Click the moment the screen turns green. Scored in milliseconds.
Precision
20 small targets with no clock. The closer to the center, the more points.
Tracking
Keep your cursor on a moving target for 30 seconds.
Flick
Hit 30 targets as fast as you can. Misses cost half a second.
